# OpenModularTurrets

Repo for [OpenModularTurrets](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/openmodularturrets) for Minecraft 1.12.2 (Forge).

Build turret bases, mount turrets on them and keep your home safe from mobs and unwanted visitors, whether you're online or offline.
The base is the brain: it handles targeting, ammo, energy, upgrades, addons and the list of trusted players for every turret mounted on it.
The mod should be fairly well documented in game; if you need any more help, ask us in our [Discord](https://discord.gg/SEG695u).

## Features

- **14 turrets**, from the disposable item turret and potato cannon to machine guns, grenade and rocket launchers, lasers, railguns and teleporter turrets
- **Turret bases in 5 tiers**; higher tiers get addon and upgrade slots
- **Choose your targets:** hostile mobs, neutral mobs, players, or any combination
- **Trusted players** with access levels, so friends can use your base without being shot
- **Upgrades and addons:** range, fire rate, accuracy, efficiency, scattershot, damage amplifier, solar panel, redstone reactor, concealer and more
- **Camouflage:** disguise turret bases as other blocks, with light value and light opacity on high tier bases
- **Memory card:** copy the settings of a base (targeting, trusted players, camouflage) onto another one
- **Generator:** burns furnace fuel and powers adjacent turret bases and other machines
- **Turret API** for other mods to add their own turrets; see [docs/turret-api.md](docs/turret-api.md)
- Integrations with JEI, HWYLA, OpenComputers and ComputerCraft

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.12.2 with Forge
- [OMLib](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/omlib) ([GitHub](https://github.com/OpenModularTurretsTeam/OMLib)), required
- [OpenModularPassiveDefense](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ompd), optional: hardened blocks, walls, fences and the camouflage trap

## Mod compatibility requests

For mod compatibility requests we have 2 requirements:

1. The mod we should add compatibility for should not be inactive
2. It should be possible to contact one of the authors directly (from dev to dev)

Changes are listed in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
